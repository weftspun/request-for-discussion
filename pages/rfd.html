RFDs

Request-for-Discussion documents: design records numbered rather than dated, so a document keeps its identity when its title changes.

RFD 1000 carries the format and the list of states a document may be in. The state on a row is what the document itself declares, and the gate in scripts/check-rfd-structure.py reads that list out of RFD 1000 rather than restating it, so the two cannot disagree.

A retraction stays in place next to what it retracts. Several documents here exist only to withdraw an earlier number, which is the point: a reader who knows which roads are dead ends is better off than one who only knows the current answer.
